# Sample: Dialogue

"You're not supposed to be here," the guard said, but his voice lacked conviction. He was young — barely twenty, Molly guessed — and his uniform hung on him like a borrowed coat.

"I know," she said. "But I'm here anyway. Are you going to stop me?"

The guard shifted his weight from one foot to the other. "I'm supposed to. It's my job."

"Is it? Your job, I mean. Or is it just what someone told you to do?"

"Don't do that."

"Do what?"

"Make everything into a question. My philosophy professor used to do that. Drove me crazy."

Molly smiled. "You went to college? What did you study?"

"Art history." The guard looked embarrassed, as if he'd admitted to a crime. "I know. Not exactly career material."

"On the contrary. I think it makes you uniquely qualified for this moment. An art historian moonlighting as a security guard, standing between me and a painting that may or may not be a forgery. You're not a guard. You're a consultant."

The guard blinked. "I'm making fourteen dollars an hour."

"Then let me make it twenty. Let me through, and I'll venmo you right now."

"That's a bribe."

"It's a consulting fee. There's a difference."

He considered this. "Is there?"

"See? Now you're doing it too."
